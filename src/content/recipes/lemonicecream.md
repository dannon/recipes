## Ingredients

- 1c heavy cream
- 1c whole milk (2% worked fine, add a tad more heavy cream I guess if so)
- .5c sugar (or a tad more)
- juice of 3 small lemons; reserve zest
- 2tsp vanilla extract
- 1tsp lemon extract
- 4 egg yolks

## Steps

1. Mix cream, milk, sugar, lemon juice, vanilla extract, and lemon extract.
2. Simmer until warm, then add 4 egg yolks, continually whisking until thickened.
3. Chill mixture, then into the ice cream machine.
4. Add zest after thickening, before finish.
