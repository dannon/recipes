## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup Texas Pete hot sauce
- 1 tablespoon red chili flakes
- 1 tablespoon white sugar
- Cracked black pepper to taste
- Salt to taste
- 1.5 cups apple cider vinegar

## Instructions

Quart Container.
Shake it.
