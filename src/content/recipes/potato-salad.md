## Ingredients

- 5 medium russet potatoes or about 2½ pounds
- 2 hard-boiled eggs
- ½ cup duke's mayonnaise
- ¼ cup yellow mustard
- ½ cup dill pickle relish
- salt and pepper

## Steps

1. Peel and cube potatoes. Add them into boiling water and continue boiling for 7–8 minutes, or until the potato chunks are fork tender.
2. Drain potatoes and place into a mixing bowl. Add salt and pepper, and stir to combine.
3. Mix mayonnaise, mustard, and pickles together for the dressing. Chop eggs fine. Pour dressing and eggs over potatoes and mix thoroughly.
